### 🎬My Favorite Movie is **Lolita**

## Overview
- **Genre:** Drama, Romance, Psychological  
- **Director:** Stanley Kubrick  
- **Release Year:** 1962  
- **Based On:** The novel **Lolita** by Vladimir Nabokov  

## What It’s About
*Lolita* tells the story of a complex and controversial relationship between a middle-aged professor and a young girl. The film explores **themes of obsession, desire, and morality** while presenting a satirical look at social taboos and human psychology.
